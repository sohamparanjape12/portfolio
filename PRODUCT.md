# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A mixed audience — recruiters at tech companies (internships and grad roles), freelance clients and startups, and fellow developers — all arriving via link share, LinkedIn, or GitHub. Context: a quick scan on desktop, often with a critical eye. They need to feel the quality of craft before they read a word of copy.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Soham Paranjape — Full-Stack Developer, CSE undergrad at MIT World Peace University, Pune. It exists to demonstrate technical depth and design sensibility in a single surface. Success means a visitor finishes the page convinced they're looking at someone who builds real things with considered taste.

## Brand Personality

Precise · Considered · Quiet

Voice is direct, without decoration. Tone is confident without bravado — the work speaks. Emotional goal: quiet authority. The interface should feel like it was made by someone who knows what they're doing and doesn't need to announce it.

## References

- **majd-portfolio.framer.website** — generous whitespace, strong sans typography, deliberate pacing
- **letsplayfight.com** — Awwwards-tier craft, smooth locomotion, spatial layout, beautiful use of scale

What specifically: deep use of white/black space as composition, premium Grotesque / Satoshi-class typography, GSAP-level motion, Locomotive Scroll smoothness, and perfect layout geometry.

## Anti-references

- **Editorial / brutalist aesthetics** — ragged grids, ink textures, torn-paper borders, high-contrast raw type
- **Over-decorated AI defaults** — cream/sand body backgrounds, glassmorphism used decoratively, gradient text, side-stripe cards, identical card grids, eyebrow labels on every section, numbered section markers as scaffold
- **Generic SaaS / startup portfolio** — hero-metric template, big numbers, gradient accents
- **Cute / illustrative** — hand-drawn icons, doodle SVGs, anything that reads as whimsical
- **Safe minimalism** — all-white, zero motion, no composition tension; minimal is not the same as boring

## Design Principles

1. **Composition over decoration** — every spatial decision (whitespace, scale, rhythm) is the design. No element earns its place by being decorative.
2. **Motion is material** — scroll-driven effects, Locomotive-class smoothness, and purposeful GSAP-level transitions are load-bearing, not afterthoughts. Every animated element has a reason.
3. **Type is the hero** — a single premium Grotesque family carries most of the visual work. Size, weight, and spacing do the job that color and pattern do not.
4. **Show, do not explain** — the portfolio demonstrates craft by being crafted. No meta-commentary, no section headers that announce "Work" as if labelling a filing cabinet.
5. **Quiet confidence** — the interface should never shout. Quality reads in restraint.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). prefers-reduced-motion must be respected on every animation — crossfade or instant as the reduced alternative. No motion-gated content.
